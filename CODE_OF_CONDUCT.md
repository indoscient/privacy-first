# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to:

* **Be respectful** – treat others with courtesy and empathy.
* **Be inclusive** – welcome people of all backgrounds and skill levels.
* **Be considerate** – recognize that our words and actions affect others.
* **Be collaborative** – focus on improving the project, not on personal attacks.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Being patient when answering questions, especially newcomers exploring
  cryptography and privacy engineering.
* Respecting differing viewpoints and acknowledging that there is more than one
  correct way to solve a problem.
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
* Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

* Harassment, intimidation, or threats of violence.
* Discriminatory remarks or jokes based on protected characteristics.
* Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* Personal insults, trolling, or demeaning language.
* Publishing private information (doxxing) without consent.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions or spamming of project channels.
* Testing exploits or attacks against the live deployment
  (`privacy.indoscient.in`) outside the responsible-disclosure process —
  see the section below.
* Any conduct that makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the
standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — including the
GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and mailing lists —
and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project
or its community in public spaces. Examples of representing the project
include using an official project e-mail address, posting via an official
social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online
or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers at **info@indoscient.in**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

**Response commitment:** reports are acknowledged within 72 hours, and the
reporter receives a status update at least weekly until the matter is
resolved.

**Confidentiality:** the identity and details of reporters will be kept
confidential to the extent possible and shared only with the maintainers
involved in investigating the report.

Maintainers are empowered to:

* Issue a private warning to the offending party.
* Temporarily suspend or permanently ban contributors from the project
  spaces.
* Remove or edit offending content (comments, issues, pull requests).
* Refuse or revoke repository access and revoke Pages/CI privileges
  associated with the project.

We reserve the right to take any other action we deem appropriate, including
escalation to GitHub staff or reporting to law enforcement when necessary.

Maintainers who violate this Code of Conduct are held to the same standards
and face the same consequences as any other community member.

## Related Policies

* **Security vulnerabilities:** do not report technical vulnerabilities
  through this channel or in public issues. Follow the responsible-disclosure
  process in [SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md).
* **Contribution standards:** development workflow and expectations are
  described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
